About VOUCHPOINT

A fair share of every sale.

VOUCHPOINT is a cashback and rewards platform for shoppers in India, run from Manipur. We exist to make sure the commission retailers pay for your purchase doesn't all stay with the middleman.

What we do

We send shoppers to stores. Stores pay us. We pay you.

Online retailers pay a commission to websites that send them customers. When you click through VOUCHPOINT and buy something, the retailer pays us that commission.

We keep part of it to run the service and give the rest back to you as cashback. That's our whole business model. You never pay us a fee, and the price you pay the retailer stays the same.

Because the money comes from the retailer, cashback is only paid once the retailer confirms your order. We explain every rule openly in our guide and our Cashback Policy.

Our promises

How we run VOUCHPOINT.

We only list offers we have set up with the retailer, with the real rate and real conditions. If we have no offer for a store, we don't pretend to.

Rules in plain words

A real person to write to
