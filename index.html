The cashback lifecycle

Always know where your money is.

Every purchase moves through the same clear stages. Here is what each one means.

  1. 1

    Tracked

    The retailer has reported your order to us.

  2. 2

    Pending

    Waiting for the store's return window to close.

  3. 3

    Approved

    The retailer has confirmed the sale. Your cashback is available.

  4. 4

    Withdrawn

    Sent to your UPI ID once your balance reaches ₹100.

Quick answers

Questions shoppers ask first.

More questions
Is VOUCHPOINT free to use?

Yes. You never pay VOUCHPOINT anything. Retailers pay us a commission when you buy through our links, and we pass part of it back to you as cashback.

How do I withdraw my cashback?

Once your cashback is approved and your available balance reaches ₹100, you can withdraw it to your own UPI ID. UPI is the only withdrawal method we offer.

Why is my cashback still pending?

Retailers confirm an order only after their return and cancellation window has closed. Until they confirm it, the cashback stays pending. This is normal and can take several weeks depending on the retailer.

My purchase didn't track. What can I do?

Contact us within the time limit set out in our Cashback Policy with your order ID, order date, store and amount. We will raise it with the retailer.

Have a question before you shop?

Write to us and we'll help you understand how cashback works for your order.
