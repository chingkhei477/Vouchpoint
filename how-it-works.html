The complete guide

From click to UPI, explained.

Cashback works because retailers pay us a commission for sending them customers. We share that commission with you. Here is exactly how a purchase becomes money in your account.

Step by step

Six steps, one rule: click first.

The single most important thing is to start your shopping trip from a VOUCHPOINT link. Everything else follows from that.

Browse offers
  1. 01

    Choose an offer

    Open the Offers page, pick a store and read its rate and conditions. The conditions tell you what does and does not earn cashback.

  2. 02

    Click Activate cashback

    This opens the retailer's website through our tracked link. The retailer now knows the visit came from VOUCHPOINT.

  3. 03

    Complete your purchase

    Shop and pay as you normally would, in the same visit. Don't leave and come back later through another website.

  4. 04

    Order tracked

    When the retailer reports your order to us, it appears as tracked and then pending. Reporting can take a few hours or a few days.

  5. 05

    Retailer approves

    After the return or cancellation window has closed, the retailer confirms the sale and your cashback becomes approved.

  6. 06

    Withdraw to UPI

    Once your approved balance is ₹100 or more, request a withdrawal to a UPI ID in your own name.

Eligibility

What earns cashback, and what doesn't.

Each offer can add its own conditions. When an offer's terms differ from this list, the offer's terms apply.

Usually eligible

  • Orders placed right after clicking a VOUCHPOINT link
  • Orders that are delivered and kept
  • Products and payment methods allowed by the offer terms
  • Coupon codes listed on the offer itself

Usually not eligible

  • Cancelled, returned, exchanged or undelivered orders
  • Orders where another cashback site, coupon site or extension took over tracking
  • Gift cards, taxes, shipping fees, and amounts paid with wallets or points
  • Orders that break the retailer's or our terms

Tracking

Help your purchase track properly.

When you click our link, the retailer stores a small tracking record (usually a cookie) in your browser. When you buy, the retailer reads it and credits the sale to VOUCHPOINT. Anything that removes or replaces that record can stop your cashback from tracking.

Tracking depends on the retailer's systems, which we don't control. That's why we can't guarantee every order will track, but we will always help you claim a missing one.

Tracking checklist

  • Allow cookies for the retailer's site, and avoid private or incognito windows.
  • Pause ad blockers and privacy extensions while you shop.
  • Start with an empty cart and add items after clicking our link.
  • Buy in the same visit. If you leave and return later, click our link again first.
  • Keep your order confirmation in case you need to report missing cashback.

Statuses & approval

What each status means.

Approval timing is set by the retailer, not by VOUCHPOINT. We update your status as soon as the retailer tells us.

TrackedThe retailer has reported your order. It moves to Pending straight away.
PendingWaiting for the retailer to confirm. This lasts at least until the return or cancellation window has closed. Pending cashback can't be withdrawn.
ApprovedThe retailer has confirmed the sale and paid us. The cashback is added to your available balance.
WithdrawnYou requested a withdrawal and we've sent it to your UPI ID.
DeclinedThe retailer rejected the order, for example because it was cancelled or returned, or it broke the offer terms.

Withdrawal

Getting your cashback out.

UPI only

Withdrawals are paid to a UPI ID registered in your own name. We don't pay to bank accounts, wallets or gift cards.

₹100 minimum

You can request a withdrawal once your approved balance is ₹100 or more. Pending cashback doesn't count.

Checked before paying

We may verify your identity or orders before paying, to protect you and stop misuse. Double-check your UPI ID before you submit.

Missing cashback

Order didn't track? Tell us.

If your order doesn't appear within 7 days of purchase, send us a missing cashback claim. We'll take it up with the retailer. The retailer makes the final decision on whether it is paid.

Report missing cashback

Include these details

  • Store name and order ID
  • Order date and the amount you paid
  • A screenshot of the order confirmation (you can attach it when your email app opens)
  • The email address you use with VOUCHPOINT

FAQ

Everything else you might ask.

Do I pay anything to use VOUCHPOINT?

No. The service is free. Cashback comes from the commission the retailer pays us, not from you.

Is the price I pay any different?

No. Going through our link does not change the price the retailer charges you.

Can I use a coupon code as well?

Only coupon codes listed on the offer. Codes from other websites can stop the retailer from crediting the sale to us, and then no cashback is paid.

How long until my cashback is approved?

It depends on the retailer. Most only confirm after their return or cancellation window ends, and some take longer to report. Where a retailer shares its usual confirmation time, we show it on the offer.

Why was my cashback amount different from what I expected?

Cashback is calculated on the final value the retailer reports, which usually excludes taxes, shipping, gift wrap and any part paid with gift cards, wallets or rewards points. Partial returns also reduce it.

Can I withdraw to a bank account or wallet?

Not at the moment. Withdrawals are paid only by UPI, to a UPI ID in your own name, with a minimum of ₹100.

What if my withdrawal fails?

If a UPI payment fails or is reversed, for example because the UPI ID is wrong, the amount goes back to your available balance and you can request it again with the correct details.
